I am a software engineer. I get to work on cool and interesting problems. I build things that I am proud to put out in the world. I like coming to work because I get to spend most of my time doing fun design and development. I don’t spend much time or energy slogging through tech debt, cleaning up bugs, waiting for things to be ready, getting changes deployed, resolving a production issue, etc.
